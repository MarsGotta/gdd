The layer's commands

Commands to look up how your changes stand. They only note the call in the layer's journal, and none of them touches your code or what was approved.

Execute, stop 3 of 3

CommandWhat it does
.gdd/gdd nextShows how the project stands and the next thing on the list.
.gdd/gdd next <id>Says a change's next step.
.gdd/gdd waitingLists the questions waiting for a person.
.gdd/gdd color --paths <paths>Says the color a change would get from the paths it touches.
.gdd/gdd color <id>Says the color of what a change touched.
.gdd/gdd limits <id>Shows how much of its limit a change has used.
.gdd/gdd metrics --all --summarySums up how your changes ended, their times and the checks' failures.
.gdd/gdd zonesChecks which files each zone covers and warns of gaps.
.gdd/gdd verify --allChecks that the logs are whole. It fails with exit code 1 if something is broken.
.gdd/gdd --versionShows the version of the project's layer.

An example

$ .gdd/gdd next
in use, waiting for a verdict: cart, 16 days 23 hours; expected: one cart in five holds two or more products
next in the backlog: catalog-pages (yellow, todo): The catalog in pages

Before you cross: Does .gdd/gdd verify --all fail if a hash doesn't match?

That's not it. A broken log, a hash that doesn't match or a step out of order make it fail.
