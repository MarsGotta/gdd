Glossary

Every GDD term, in one line.

Look up, stop 1 of 4

The method

Gate
What a change has to get through to go in, whatever the agent says.
Check
The way a gate is validated: machine, reviewer or person.
Standing checks
The ones that hold for the whole project and that every change goes through.
The five moments
Prepare, Brief, Execute, Close and Harden.
Core
What GDD doesn't negotiate and you can demand of any engine.
Context
What you give the agent so it can get through a gate. It helps, but it proves nothing.

The project

Blueprint
The project's rules: gates, zones, colors, models and limits.
Constitution
A short document with what must never happen in the product.
Zone
Paths in the project with a color, by the damage they can do.
Color
The risk of a change: green, yellow or red, by the most delicate thing it touches.
Limits
The rounds, minutes and cost a change can spend.
Level
Light, standard or deep: how much the model reasons in each job.
Debt
A check that already fails without the change, with its baseline.

A change

Brief
What a change has to meet, written before anyone builds.
Rule
Something that has to be true in the product, said in one sentence.
Scenario
A concrete example of a rule, in Gherkin, that ends up as a test.
Seal
The brief, scenarios and tests, kept before anyone builds.
Cases
The tests of the change, one per scenario, seen failing before anyone builds.
Round
One pass of building and going through the gates.
In use
A change already on the main branch, not yet closed.
Verdict
Your answer at Close: yes, not yet or no.
What slips through
A failure that got past every gate. Harden turns it into a check.

The roles

Role
A job in a change that a separate agent does.
Orchestrator
The agent that hands out the work and builds nothing.
Agent
The one that builds the change and gets through the gates.
Reviewer
The agent that reviews with a clean context and answers pass, fix or stop.
Skill
A file of instructions the agent loads when it needs it.

The guarantees

Layer
The gdd command, which keeps the order, the limits and the log. It isn't an engine.
Log
Everything that happened in a change, one event per line.
Degrees
How far what judges the agent is out of its reach.
Engine
What enforces the core without depending on the agent. Any engine that meets it will do; T-minus is still in development.
Hidden examples
Extra examples of the scenarios, kept away from the builder.
Ready-made rules
Gates and checks ready for each area. Not published yet.
