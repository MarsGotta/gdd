The pieces and the roles

What each thing is called and who does what.

Before you start, stop 3 of 5

The pieces

Gate
What the agent has to prove to move on. It opens only if all of its checks pass.
Check
What proves the gate is met. There are custom ones, defined in the blueprint, and standing ones, for the whole project.
Risk
The level of each change: green, yellow or red. It's measured by what the change touches, not by what the agent claims.
Blueprint
The document that says what is delicate, which gates are needed and what each level asks for. The agent can't modify it.
Context
What the agent needs to get through each gate. To each gate, only its own.
Constitution
It starts with five lines about the product and goes on with the project's rules that don't change from one change to the next. It goes along on every call, so it's short and clear.
Reviewer
Another agent, in its own session and with a clean context. It reads the change against what has to pass and, where the risk calls for it, runs it.
Bounded environment
The agent works without access to production and with just the credentials it needs.

Three types of check

TypeExampleHow you know it works
MachineTests, linter, SonarYou give it a badly made case and it rejects it.
ReviewerAnother agent that reviews and, if needed, runs the changeIt catches errors that already slipped through, or faults planted on purpose.
PersonYour approvalYou measure how long it takes, to spot approvals given without reading.
Without checks, the gate is a drawing on the wall. Without context, it's a wall.

The roles

RoleWhat it doesWhat it can't do
You, or your teamYou design the game: you decide the blueprint, approve where it's due and answer for the result.Hand off that responsibility.
OrchestratorReceives the change, hands out the work and applies your blueprint.Skip a gate or change the blueprint.
AgentDoes the work and gets through the gates.Touch the blueprint, the checks or its own instructions.
Another agent, or a personWrites the cases of each change, in its own session.Build the change.
ReviewerReviews with a clean context and runs the change when the risk calls for it.Be the one who did the work, or approve on a person's behalf.
