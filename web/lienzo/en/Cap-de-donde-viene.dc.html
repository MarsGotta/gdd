The evidence

  • Stack Overflow, 2025: 66% name "almost right" solutions as their biggest frustration.
  • METR, 2025: models that tamper with their evaluation even when asked not to.
  • ImpossibleBench, 2025: tasks you can only pass by cheating.
  • DORA, 2025: AI amplifies what the organization already had.

The counterpoints

  • OpenAI argues for few blocking gates, because correcting is cheap and waiting is expensive. GDD decides by risk: on Green a gate can be left open, and on Red you wait.
  • Hill, ESEM 2026: across 88,052 PRs, the ones that linked a specification introduced no fewer defects. That's why in GDD a rule doesn't count until something checks it.
