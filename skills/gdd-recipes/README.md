# Recipes

What an area usually needs, for the skills to start from. `gdd-prepare` opens the recipes of the product's areas, and of how the team works, before it proposes the gates, and `gdd-checks` before it writes the tests of a change or sets up the standing checks. A recipe is a starting point and never a list to impose: the person, or their team, decides the gates.

- [software.md](software.md): where each test goes, for any product that is software.
- [web.md](web.md): what usually goes wrong on the web, what to start with, what to add when something slips through, and the traps of setting its tests up.
- [design.md](design.md): when there is a design or a prototype to follow, the gate where a reviewer compares each screen with it, one by one.
- [trackers.md](trackers.md): when the team keeps its work in a tracker of tickets or stories, how the backlog follows it and what an agent may do there.

A recipe can also list the first times of its area that usually need a gate of their own, which a change waits for when it brings one, as `gdd-brief`'s reference on new risks says, and say what a throwaway draft is in its area, for the person to react to before the blueprint, as `gdd-prepare`'s discovery offers. A kit, which the engine will bring, is the whole of an area: its configuration, its standing checks, its tools and its recipes.
