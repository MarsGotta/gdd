# Researching for the person

Research similar products and the sector's rules when it helps the person decide what is delicate or which gate a zone needs.

- **Only proven sources.** Official documentation, the regulation itself, the vendor's own pages before third-party posts.
- **Always cited, always copied.** Each source goes to `gdd/context/` as a copy with its source and date (`assets/context.md`), and the blueprint says which zone or gate gets it.
- **The person decides.** What you found helps them choose; it doesn't choose for them. When it touches a law or a regulation, say that it isn't legal advice.
- **What it turns into.** A finding is useful when it becomes a zone, a gate, a scenario or the context of a gate. If it becomes none of those, it doesn't go in the blueprint.
